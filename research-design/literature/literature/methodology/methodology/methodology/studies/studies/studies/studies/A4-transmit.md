# A4 — TRANSMIT

## From Biodiversity Disclosure to Financial Materiality
### Information Quality and the Pricing of Nature-Related Risk

**Scientific function:** Test the information-to-finance transmission mechanism

---

## Research Question

> Does biodiversity disclosure quality modify the extent to which ecological
> exposure is incorporated into financial decisions?

## Core Mechanism

The study focuses on:

**Ecological Exposure × Information Quality → Financial Outcome**

The objective is not simply to test whether biodiversity is associated with firm
performance.

Instead, the study investigates the mechanism through which ecological exposure
becomes financially visible.

## Theoretical Foundations

- Information asymmetry
- Corporate disclosure
- Decision usefulness
- Nature-related financial risk

## Hypotheses

**H4a.** Higher biodiversity exposure is associated with less favourable financing
conditions when that exposure is observable.

**H4b.** The relationship between ecological exposure and financial outcomes varies
with biodiversity disclosure quality.

**H4c.** Information-transmission effects are stronger where initial information
environments are weaker or nature dependence is higher.

## Financial Outcomes

One primary outcome will be selected before confirmatory analysis.

Potential outcomes include:

- loan spreads;
- cost of debt;
- financing conditions;
- credit risk;
- documented market-based outcomes.

## Empirical Strategy

Potential specifications include:

- panel fixed effects;
- Exposure × Disclosure Quality interactions;
- lag structures;
- high-dimensional fixed effects;
- information or regulatory shocks where credible.

Without credible exogenous variation, findings will be presented as conditional
associations rather than causal effects.

## Position in the Project

**IDENTIFY → TRANSMIT → INTEGRATE**

A3 investigates information production.

A4 investigates whether that information changes the financial transmission of
ecological exposure.

A5 integrates the resulting information layers.
