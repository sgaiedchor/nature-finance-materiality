# A2 — MEASURE

## Beyond ESG Scores
### AI-Driven Climate and Biodiversity Risk in Corporate Sustainability

**Scientific function:** Measure ecological materiality and informational misalignment

**Research stage:** Empirical measurement and validation

---

## Research Question

> To what extent do conventional ESG indicators capture firms' underlying
> climate- and biodiversity-related exposure, and what incremental information
> is provided by ecological and textual measures?

## Scientific Motivation

Corporate ESG indicators provide useful sustainability information, but aggregate
scores may not fully represent firms' dependencies on ecosystems, biodiversity
exposure, physical climate risks or location-specific ecological pressures.

This study therefore examines the potential gap between:

**Underlying Ecological Exposure**

and

**Observable Sustainability Information**

## Theoretical Foundations

- Information asymmetry
- Corporate disclosure
- Legitimacy theory
- Natural capital and ecosystem dependencies

## Hypotheses

**H2a.** Higher ecological exposure is associated with greater financial
vulnerability, conditional on conventional firm characteristics.

**H2b.** Aggregate ESG indicators do not fully explain heterogeneity in firms'
underlying ecological exposure.

**H2c.** Ecological and textual information provides incremental explanatory or
predictive information beyond conventional financial and ESG indicators.

## Information Architecture

Potential inputs include:

- financial indicators;
- ESG information;
- sustainability reports;
- biodiversity dependencies;
- ecosystem-service exposure;
- climate-risk information;
- textual sustainability signals;
- geospatial information where reliable.

## Empirical Strategy

The study is primarily concerned with measurement and validation rather than
causal identification.

Methods may include:

- panel models;
- fixed effects;
- incremental information tests;
- NLP-based information extraction;
- benchmark comparisons;
- temporal out-of-sample validation;
- model ablation.

## Expected Contribution

A2 develops reusable measures of ecological exposure and informational alignment.

These measures provide the empirical bridge between ecological materiality and
corporate disclosure.

## Position in the Project

**STRUCTURE → MEASURE → IDENTIFY**

A1 identifies the knowledge gap.

A2 tests whether conventional corporate information adequately represents
ecological exposure.

A3 subsequently examines whether reporting institutions can reduce the identified
information gap.
