# A5 — INTEGRATE

## Nature-Related Sustainability Intelligence
### Integrating Ecological Exposure, Disclosure Quality and Financial Materiality

**Scientific function:** Integrate and validate the Nature-to-Finance information architecture

---

## Research Question

> Does an integrated multi-source architecture combining ecological exposure,
> disclosure quality, governance and financial information provide incremental
> and robust decision value relative to conventional ESG-based approaches?

## Scientific Objective

A5 does not seek to construct another generic ESG score.

It tests whether information generated throughout the research program can be
combined to improve decision-relevant analysis.

## Information Architecture

### Ecological Layer

- biodiversity exposure;
- ecosystem dependencies;
- environmental pressures;
- location-sensitive information.

### Informational Layer

- ESG indicators;
- disclosure quality;
- corporate reports;
- NLP/LLM-derived signals.

### Governance Layer

- sustainability governance;
- oversight structures;
- relevant corporate-governance indicators.

### Financial Layer

- accounting information;
- financing conditions;
- risk measures;
- financial outcomes.

## Hypotheses

**H5a.** The integrated architecture provides incremental out-of-sample decision
value relative to conventional ESG-based benchmarks.

**H5b.** Any improvement derives from complementary information across data blocks.

**H5c.** Incremental performance remains observable under temporal and, where
feasible, sectoral or geographic distribution shifts.

## Validation

Evaluation may include:

- temporal holdout;
- rolling validation;
- benchmark models;
- feature-block ablation;
- calibration;
- stability;
- distribution-shift tests;
- interpretability;
- missing-data sensitivity;
- leakage controls.

## Role of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence is treated as an analytical instrument rather than an
assumed scientific contribution.

Complex models must demonstrate incremental value relative to transparent statistical
benchmarks.

A failure to outperform simpler approaches remains a scientifically informative result.
