# A3 — IDENTIFY

## Double Materiality in Action
### Does European Sustainability Reporting Improve Biodiversity Disclosure?

**Scientific function:** Identify the institutional effect of sustainability reporting

**Research stage:** Causal identification where design assumptions permit

---

## Research Question

> Do sustainability-reporting requirements improve the quality, specificity
> and ecological alignment of corporate biodiversity disclosure?

## Scientific Motivation

More sustainability disclosure does not necessarily imply better information.

The study therefore distinguishes:

**Disclosure Volume**

from

**Disclosure Quality**

from

**Ecological Alignment**

## Theoretical Foundations

- Institutional theory
- Information asymmetry
- Corporate disclosure
- Legitimacy theory

Double materiality is treated as a regulatory and reporting framework rather than
as an independent academic theory.

## Hypotheses

**H3a.** Stronger sustainability-reporting requirements increase biodiversity
disclosure quality and specificity.

**H3b.** The effect is stronger among firms with greater underlying nature
dependence or ecological impact.

**H3c.** Reporting requirements reduce the gap between independently measured
ecological exposure and corporate biodiversity disclosure.

## Biodiversity Disclosure Quality Index

Potential dimensions include:

1. dependencies;
2. impacts;
3. risks;
4. opportunities;
5. location specificity;
6. value-chain information;
7. targets;
8. quantitative metrics;
9. governance;
10. financial consequences.

## Identification Strategy

Subject to regulatory and data validation, potential approaches include:

- Difference-in-Differences;
- staggered-treatment estimators;
- event studies;
- Triple Differences based on nature dependence.

Causal language will only be used where the identifying assumptions are supported.

## Regulatory Context

Treatment definitions and timing will be based on the applicable regulatory framework
at the time of empirical implementation.

The design will therefore document regulatory scope, implementation dates,
exemptions and relevant amendments before treatment assignment.

## Position in the Project

**MEASURE → IDENTIFY → TRANSMIT**

A2 measures the ecological-information gap.

A3 examines whether institutional intervention can reduce that gap.

A4 tests whether the resulting information matters for financial decisions.
