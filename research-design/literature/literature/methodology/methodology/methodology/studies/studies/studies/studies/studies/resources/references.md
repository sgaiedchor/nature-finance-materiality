## Foundational Academic Literature

### Information Asymmetry and Disclosure

Healy, P. M., & Palepu, K. G. (2001).  
*Information asymmetry, corporate disclosure, and the capital markets.*

### Stakeholder Theory

Freeman, R. E. (1984).  
*Strategic Management: A Stakeholder Approach.*

### Legitimacy Theory

Suchman, M. C. (1995).  
*Managing legitimacy: Strategic and institutional approaches.*

### Institutional Theory

DiMaggio, P. J., & Powell, W. W. (1983).  
*The Iron Cage Revisited: Institutional Isomorphism and Collective Rationality
in Organizational Fields.*

### Natural Capital and Biodiversity Economics

Dasgupta, P. (2021).  
*The Economics of Biodiversity: The Dasgupta Review.*
