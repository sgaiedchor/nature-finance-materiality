# A1 — STRUCTURE

## Biodiversity, Climate and Finance Nexus

**Scientific function:** Structure the research field

**Status:** Research manuscript

### Research Question

How has the intellectual structure of the biodiversity–climate–finance nexus
evolved over time, and does the literature exhibit increasing structural integration
between nature-related and financial research?

### Data

- Web of Science
- 177 publications
- 1998–2026
- 103 sources

### Methods

- Bibliometric mapping
- Co-occurrence networks
- Thematic evolution
- Shannon entropy
- Tsallis entropy
- Jensen–Shannon divergence
- Temporal Nature–Finance Coupling Index (TNFCI)
- Null-model validation

### Contribution

The study distinguishes growth in publication activity from genuine structural
integration between nature-related and financial research.

### Position in the Project

**STRUCTURE → MEASURE**

The study identifies the knowledge and measurement gaps subsequently investigated
at firm level in A2.
